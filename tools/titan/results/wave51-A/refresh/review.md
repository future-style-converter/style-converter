## Flagged sheets and the decision

- **Android__023_Shadow_Simple.png** (unsure, glyph-mask-A): 57 red px inside the new label band (y 6..12): +1-byte background brightening ((26,26,46)->(27,27,47)) immediately right of vertical strokes of H, W, M, P; label ink mask itself pixel-identical to web/iOS, invisible to the eye, no component paint affected. Android-only and only on blurred box-shadow stems (023/024/025 plus 093_Avatar_Circle outside this lane; absent on 027 spread-only) — looks like the Android band label is composited through the component's blurred-shadow layer at a sub-pixel offset. Outside design-record §5's allowed red set, so flagged for decision.

- **Android__024_Shadow_Colored.png** (unsure, glyph-mask-A): 54 red px inside the label band, same +1-byte halo right of glyph strokes (H, W, L, D) as Android 023; ink mask identical to web/iOS, no component change. Same Android blurred-shadow-only pattern; outside the allowed red set.

- **Android__025_Shadow_Multiple.png** (unsure, glyph-mask-A): 53 red px inside the label band, same +1-byte halo right of glyph strokes (H, W, L, T, E) as Android 023; ink mask identical to web/iOS, no component change. Same Android blurred-shadow-only pattern; outside the allowed red set.

- **Android__093_Avatar_Circle** (defect, glyph-mask-B): Fresh label ink is P-1 ((173,173,179)/(173,173,180)) on 46 glyph px over the plain ground (not under component paint) for glyphs V, 2nd-A first column, R, R, C (x 14..21, 38..43, 63..72), each with a +1 lift (26,26,46)->(27,27,47) in the column immediately right = 48 red px inside the band rows 6..12; a horizontal 1-px sub-pixel bleed that fails glyph-pixel==P (S4b). Mask positions are identical x3 and web/iOS are exact P; the committed Android baseline shows the same bleed beside the old label's T stem (x=48), so it is an Android-only raster property of this stem, not a mask misplacement.

- **web__046_Perspective_Rotate** (unsure, glyph-mask-B): Besides the old-label row, two 1-px AA edge columns of the perspective-rotated box changed over rows 16..55 (80 px): x=19 (110,67,135)->(110,67,134) and x=62 (114,69,139)->(110,67,134), the right edge now byte-equal to the left; no column moved (x=18/63 ground, x=20/61 full fill in both), so it is a coverage re-rasterisation of the 3D layer after its label child left it, but it lies outside regions (i)+(ii). iOS/Android 046 show only the old-label row.

**Decision: refresh.** None of the five is a defect of the chrome:
- Android 023/024/025/093: a +1-LSB lift on the background column immediately right of vertical glyph strokes, and on 093 the ink itself at 173 instead of 174 on 46 glyph pixels. Android-only, only on stems whose box-shadow is BLURRED (absent on 027 spread-only), invisible to the eye, and the committed Android baselines already carry the same bleed beside the OLD label's strokes — a sub-pixel resampling property of Android's capture on those stems, not a placement or colour error of the chrome (mask positions identical ×3; web and iOS exact). All four are glyph-mask stems, where the tripwire asserts position only. Recorded as a BACKLOG residual.
- web 046_Perspective_Rotate: two 1-px anti-aliased edge columns of the perspective-rotated box changed by 1–5 levels with no column moving (x=18/63 ground, x=20/61 full fill before and after): the 3D layer re-rasterised its coverage once the label child left it — the old label's side effect on a transformed layer, which §5 allows.
